Common Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about our ensemble, programs, and booking.

The Kudyapi is an ancestral Philippine two-stringed fretted boat lute carved from soft wood, symbolizing traditional Filipino melodic heritage.

Additionally, in our choir, KUDYAPI forms an acronym reflecting our holistic arts education: KU (Kultura - Culture), D (Dance), Y (and), A (Art), and PI (Pilipinas).

We welcome children, adolescents, and young adults between the ages of 5 and 23.

While our founding mission was designed to connect 2nd and 3rd generation Filipinos born and raised in Spain with their cultural heritage, we warmly invite youth of any background who have a genuine passion for singing, learning Filipino culture, and performing in an ensemble.

No prior formal musical training is required. What matters most is enthusiasm, commitment, and a willingness to learn!

During auditions, our directors assess basic pitch matching, rhythmic sense, and vocal range in a warm, welcoming, and encouraging environment.

Rehearsals are held on weekends (Saturdays and Sundays) at our cultural center premises affiliated with Centro Filipino in Spain.

Specific rehearsal schedules are communicated to members upon enrollment, with additional sessions scheduled leading up to major concert tours and festivals.

Beyond multi-part vocal harmonization, members receive instruction in:

  • Ukulele and acoustic guitar
  • Piano and vocal accompaniment
  • Traditional Philippine folk dancing (Tinikling, Pandanggo sa Ilaw, Sayaw sa Bangko)
  • Stage presence, theatrical expression, and Tagalog language diction

To invite or book Coro Kudyapi for international music festivals, church liturgies, embassy functions, or civic galas, please complete our Booking Contact Form.

We recommend reaching out at least 4 to 8 weeks in advance to coordinate travel, logistics, and customized repertoire selection.

Coro Kudyapi is a non-profit cultural initiative operating with the volunteer dedication of its directors, parents, and community patrons. We welcome financial sponsorships, instrument donations, and tour support.

To become an official sponsor or supporter, please contact our management team.

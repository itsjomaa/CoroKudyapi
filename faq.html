Questions & Answers

Common inquiries regarding auditions, joining the choir, rehearsals, and booking.

The Kudyapi is an ancient Filipino two-stringed boat lute. It also represents our choir's acronym: KU (Kultura), D (Dance), Y (and), A (Art), and PI (Pilipinas).

We welcome children and youth from ages 5 to 23. While formed to help Filipino youth born in Spain discover their cultural roots, we warmly embrace young people of all backgrounds who love singing and music.

No prior formal training is required. We evaluate enthusiasm, pitch matching, and commitment in a friendly, supportive environment.

Rehearsals take place on weekends in Spain at our Centro Filipino premises, covering vocal harmonies, instruments (guitar, ukulele, piano), and traditional choreography.

You can send an event inquiry via our Contact page with proposed dates and concert details.

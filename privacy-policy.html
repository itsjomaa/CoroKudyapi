Privacy Policy

How we protect, respect, and manage your personal data

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective Date: September 25, 2026

Overview: Coro Kudyapi ("we", "our", or "the choir") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of our visitors, members, parents, and community supporters. This policy details how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EU 2016/679) and the Spanish Organic Law on Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD 3/2018).

1. Data Controller

The entity responsible for processing your personal data on this website is:

  • Entity: Coro Kudyapi (Cultural Youth Choir, affiliated with Centro Filipino)
  • Location: Barcelona & Madrid, Spain
  • Email for Privacy Inquiries: privacy@corokudyapi.com

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Contact & Audition Information: When you submit our contact or audition form, we collect your name, email address, telephone number, candidate age, and any comments or musical background details you submit.
  • Parental/Guardian Details: When applicants are minors under 14 or 16 years of age, we collect and require parent or guardian verification.
  • Browsing & Technical Data: Anonymized IP addresses, browser types, screen resolutions, referring URLs, and pages visited, collected through cookies and performance tools if consented to.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Answering Inquiries & Booking Requests: Handled based on our mutual pre-contractual relationship or legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(b) & (f) GDPR).
  • Choir Audition Management: Processing youth enrollments, scheduling audition appointments, and communicating with applicants or parents based on explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Website Analytics & Performance: Analyzing visitor traffic to enhance performance and user experience, strictly based on voluntary consent given through our cookie consent tool (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Legal Compliance: Fulfilling statutory obligations under Spanish and European regulations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

4. Protection of Minors

Given that Coro Kudyapi works with children and adolescents aged 5 to 23, we take utmost care regarding the privacy of minors. Any audition submission or registration for individuals under 14 years old in Spain requires the express verification and consent of a parent or legal guardian. Photographs and performance recordings of choir members are only published with written authorization from parents or guardians.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies (small text files stored in your browser) to ensure core navigation functions and, with your consent, to measure anonymous visitor traffic through Google Analytics.

6. Third-Party Services & Embedded Content

Our website utilizes trustworthy third-party services:

  • YouTube IFrame API: For streaming concert recordings. When you play a video, YouTube/Google may process technical data according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics 4: Configured in restricted consent mode, ensuring analytics cookies are only stored when approved by the visitor.
  • Font Awesome & Google Fonts: For typography and icon display with privacy-preserving font caching.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected (such as handling audition cohorts or booking inquiries), or as required by applicable legal retention mandates. Form submissions are deleted or anonymized once the inquiry is resolved.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation, you possess the right to:

  • Access: Request confirmation of whether we process your data and receive a copy.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data when no longer needed.
  • Restriction: Request limitation of processing under specific circumstances.
  • Data Portability: Receive your provided data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke any previously granted consent at any time without retroactive penalties.

To exercise any of these rights, please write to us at privacy@corokudyapi.com. You also have the right to lodge a formal complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

9. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically revise this Privacy Policy to reflect modifications in our activities, security standards, or legislative requirements. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.

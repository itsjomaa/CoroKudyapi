How to Manage Cookies via Your Browser

In addition to our on-page controls above, most modern web browsers allow you to control cookies through their browser settings. You can delete existing cookies, block specific domains, or prevent all cookies from being stored.

Google Chrome

Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → Manage exceptions and site data.

Apple Safari

Settings → Safari → Advanced → Block All Cookies or Website Data.

Mozilla Firefox

Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection → Cookies.

Microsoft Edge

Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data.

About Us

The Kudyapi Choir is a musical ensemble dedicated to singing, dancing, acting and playing instruments such as the ukulele, guitar and piano. It is composed of 42 members between the ages of 5 and 23. The goal of the choir is for 3rd generation Filipinos, born and raised in Spain, to be able to learn, appreciate and become more familiar with the culture of the Philippines through music.

The name “Kudyapi” refers to a traditional two-stringed instrument from the Philippines. Besides, it also stands for: KU (Kultura), D (dance), Y (and), A (Art) and PI (Pilipinas).

We have a vision that focuses mainly on spreading our talent and the richness of our culture until we can reach a professional level as a choir in the world of music. In addition, the development and creative growth of each member is promoted.
